Honey garlic chicken

Recipe · 25 min

25min4servings7ingredients

Sticky, sweet-savory chicken thighs made in one pan. The glaze is just honey, garlic and soy.

Ingredients tap to tick

  • 4 chicken thighs
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • Sesame + scallion
  1. 0:12Sear the thighs skin-down until golden
  2. 0:34Add garlic, then pour in the glaze
  3. 0:51Simmer until sticky, about 5 min

Rook Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy covers the Rook app and withrook.app. Rook saves videos and posts you share to it from TikTok, YouTube and Instagram. It breaks them down with AI and brings them back to you later. This page explains what data Rook uses, who processes it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account info: your email address, plus your name if you sign in with Apple or Google.
  • Your saves: the links you share to Rook, and the notes, albums, reminders and Ask questions you create.
  • Breakdowns of your saves: to break a save down, Rook reads the post's title, caption and details, and what is said or shown in the video (a transcript or the video itself). We keep the breakdown and use the transcript only to power search inside your own saves.
  • Searches: what you type into search, so we can find matching saves.
  • Rook Pro purchases: if you subscribe, Apple tells us your subscription, its status and when it renews or ends. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your card details.
  • Feedback: messages you send us from the app.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports and error logs from your device and our servers, so we can fix problems and keep costs under control.
  • Waitlist and withrook.app: if you join the waitlist, your first name, email, how you found us and whether a friend invited you. The site counts page activity without cookies or IP addresses (how far you scroll, which examples and questions you open, how long you stay). If you join, the activity from that visit is linked to your waitlist spot so we can see what helped you decide.
  • How you use the app: which screens and cards you open and which features you use, linked to your account. We use it to improve Rook and so Ask can answer questions like what you have watched. It never includes your notes, titles, links, searches or Ask text. You can turn it off any time in Account under "Help improve Rook".

We do not collect your contacts, location, photos or browsing history. We do not show ads, sell your data or track you across other apps or websites.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your saves synced across devices.
  • To create breakdowns, key points, timestamps and recipe or workout cards for each save.
  • To answer your Ask questions about your own saves, and to search your saves.
  • To send the reminders you choose.
  • To provide Rook Pro and check your subscription.

AI and other services that process your data

Rook asks for your permission before any of your saves are sent to AI services. You can say no and still save and watch; breakdowns and Ask wait until you agree.

  • Anthropic (Claude): writes breakdowns and Ask answers from your saves, notes and questions.
  • Google (Gemini): watches YouTube videos to understand them, and turns text into search data.
  • Supadata: fetches what is said in TikTok and Instagram videos, and Instagram post details.
  • Google (YouTube Data API): fetches YouTube video details.
  • Supabase: hosting, database and sign-in.
  • Sentry: receives crash reports (what crashed, device model, iOS version and app build) so we can fix crashes. It never includes your saves, notes or account details. You can turn it off in Account under "Help improve Rook".
  • Apple: sign in with Apple, purchases and subscriptions.

These services receive only what they need to do their job for Rook, and they are required to protect your data at least as well as this policy does. Rook does not store copies of the videos you save. Videos play through each platform's own player, and that platform's privacy policy applies while you watch.

Rook uses YouTube API Services. By using Rook with YouTube videos you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms), and Google's Privacy Policy applies (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Instagram

If you choose Connect Instagram, you sign in to Instagram on Instagram's own page inside Rook. That sign-in stays on your phone so Instagram videos can play in Rook. It is never sent to us. You can disconnect any time in Account.

Website and waitlist

When you join the waitlist on withrook.app we keep:

  • Your email address, to send your invite and a few updates about Rook. We never sell it or share it for marketing.
  • Where you came from: the link tag you arrived with (for example a TikTok video) and the name of the site that sent you, never the full address.
  • Your referral link and how many friends joined with it, so we can move you up the list.
  • A one-way scrambled version of your internet address, only to stop spam sign-ups. We never store the address itself.

The site also records simple, anonymous usage: which sections are seen and which buttons are tapped, with a random ID that lasts only while the tab is open. It uses no cookies, no ad trackers and no third-party analytics, and it is deleted after 180 days.

Welcome and invite emails are sent through Resend, an email delivery service.

To leave the waitlist or have your email deleted, write to hello@withrook.app.

Your saves are private

Other Rook users cannot see your saves.

How long we keep it

We keep your account, saves, breakdowns and notes until you delete them or your account. Usage data is deleted after 180 days. Diagnostics and AI processing logs are kept only as long as needed to run and fix the service, and are deleted with your account. Crash reports sent to Sentry are not linked to your account and are deleted after 30 days.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Turn off usage data in Account under "Help improve Rook".
  • Withdraw AI permission by deleting your account, or by emailing us.
  • Delete your account any time in the app under Account, then Delete account. This permanently removes your account and everything you saved. Deleting your account does not cancel a Rook Pro subscription; cancel it in your iPhone's Settings.

Children

Rook is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
